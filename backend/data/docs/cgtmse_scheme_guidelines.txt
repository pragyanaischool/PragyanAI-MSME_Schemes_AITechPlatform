CREDIT GUARANTEE FUND TRUST FOR MICRO AND SMALL ENTERPRISES (CGTMSE)
MINISTRY OF MSME & SIDBI - OPERATIONAL CIRCULAR

1. OBJECTIVE:
To provide collateral-free credit facilities (term loans and working capital) to new and existing Micro and Small Enterprises without third-party guarantees or physical collateral pledging.

2. CREDIT COVERAGE LIMITS:
Credit facilities up to INR 500 Lakhs (INR 5 Crores) per eligible enterprise. Guarantee cover extends up to 85% for Micro Enterprises for loans up to INR 5 Lakhs, and up to 75% for loans ranging from INR 5 Lakhs to INR 500 Lakhs.
Special provisions for Women-led and SC/ST-led enterprises offer enhanced guarantee coverage up to 85% across all loan slabs.

3. ANNUAL SERVICE FEE:
Concessional Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF) starting at 0.37% per annum for Micro units in manufacturing and 0.55% for regular units.

4. ELIGIBLE FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS:
Scheduled Commercial Banks (SBI, Canara Bank, Bank of Baroda, etc.), Regional Rural Banks (Prathama, Karnataka Gramin Bank), and select NBFCs registered with SIDBI. Applications can be routed directly via the national JanSamarth portal (jansamarth.in).
