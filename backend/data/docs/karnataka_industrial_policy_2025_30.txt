GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA - DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE & INDUSTRIES
POLICY DOCUMENT: KARNATAKA INDUSTRIAL POLICY 2025-2030

1. CLASSIFICATION OF TALUKS INTO ZONES:
Zone 1: Industrially backward taluks. Eligible for 35% Investment Promotion Subsidy (IPS) on the value of Fixed Assets (Plant & Machinery), up to a maximum cap of INR 35 Lakhs for Micro units and INR 60 Lakhs for Small units.
Zone 2: Industrially developing taluks. Eligible for 25% Investment Promotion Subsidy (IPS) capped at INR 25 Lakhs for Micro units.
Zone 3: Developed / Urbanized taluks including municipal limits of Bengaluru Urban. Not eligible for capital subsidies. Eligible for non-fiscal facilitation and power reliability corridors.

2. POWER TARIFF CONCESSION:
Micro & Small enterprises established in Zone 1 and Zone 2 receive an electricity duty rebate of INR 1.50 per unit (kWh) for the first 36 months from the date of commercial production.

3. STAMP DUTY & CONVERSION CHARGES:
100% reimbursement of stamp duty and land conversion fine for Micro and Small manufacturing enterprises in Zone 1.
75% reimbursement for enterprises in Zone 2.

4. APPLICATION WINDOW & NODAL DESK:
Claims must be submitted online via the Karnataka Udyog Mitra e-Udyog single-window portal (kum.karnataka.gov.in) within 6 months of commissioning commercial operations, supported by a Joint Director (DIC) field inspection report.
